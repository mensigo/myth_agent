Athena is the goddess of wisdom, skilled warfare, and crafts. She was born in an extraordinary way: Zeus swallowed the pregnant Titaness Metis, and Athena later sprang fully grown and armored from his head. A virgin goddess ("Parthenos"), she is the patron of Athens, which she won by giving the city the olive tree in her contest with Poseidon.

Athena represents war guided by discipline and strategy, in contrast to the raw violence of Ares, and she favors clever, resourceful heroes such as Odysseus, Perseus, and Heracles, aiding them with counsel and protection. She also presides over handicrafts, especially weaving; in one myth she turns the boastful mortal weaver Arachne into a spider. Her attributes are the owl, symbol of her wisdom, the olive, and the aegis bearing the head of the Gorgon Medusa.
